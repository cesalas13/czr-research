# Diligence Memo Pack

Four investment-committee-style memos on tokens my research desk analyzed and traded on a graded paper book (Robinhood chain, summer 2026). All positions referenced were paper positions with notional sizing; every number traces to the desk's contemporaneous trade logs and contract-level census files.

Each memo follows the same format: summary and verdict, asset and market context, mechanism (contract-verified where possible), holder base and flow evidence, risks and invalidation levels, decision and sizing, then outcome and post-mortem.

The picks cover the four arcs a desk actually produces:

- **HOPPY**: the model winner, +77 percent with pre-placed exits (Outcome A, Reasoning A)
- **HYPERIUM**: a graded loss where the verification artifact failed (Outcome F, Reasoning B)
- **INDEX**: a contract-level mechanism autopsy that called the unwind nine days early
- **DATABEAR**: a structural range trade broken by regime, right level, wrong context (Outcome F, Reasoning B)

The grading convention separates outcome quality from reasoning quality on purpose: a good process can lose and a bad process can win, and only the reasoning grade compounds.
