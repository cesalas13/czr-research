Virgil Research

A daily market briefing written around what you hold.

Most market newsletters send everyone the same email. Virgil starts with the big picture, then ends with your own coins: what moved, by how much, and whether anything in the data explains it.

What's in each briefing

  • Macro. Treasury yields, the VIX, the dollar, stocks, gold, oil, and copper.
  • Crypto macro. Bitcoin and Ether, total crypto market cap, stablecoin supply, and funding rates.
  • Memecoins. What's moving on Solana, Base, and Robinhood Chain, with a plain note on the risks.
  • Your holdings. A short read on each coin you own.

Every number comes from public market data. If the writing includes a number that isn't in the data, the briefing throws it out. When the data doesn't show why something moved, it says so.

From a test briefing, October 13, 2026

At the last close, the US 10-year yield was 5.24% and the two-year was 4.8%, both higher on the day, while the Nasdaq slipped 0.44% and the S&P was down 0.06%. Total crypto market cap fell 2.67% over the past day, with Bitcoin at $83,334 and Ether at $2,509. Stablecoin supply is down 0.51% over seven days.
