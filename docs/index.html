Research

Cesar Salas

I studied finance and taught myself to code.

This year I've been building a research tool for crypto markets. It tracks tokens, writes up what it finds, and checks later whether it was right. This site is where that work lives: the dashboards, the writeups, and the calls that went wrong.

Next I'm turning it into a newsletter that's personal to what each reader owns.

I'm in the East Village. Email cesalas13@gmail.com.
